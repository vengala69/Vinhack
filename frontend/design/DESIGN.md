## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intelligent, restorative, and high-performance environment tailored for ambitious students navigating intense academic demands. The visual aesthetic fuses high-end dark mode minimalism with atmospheric glassmorphic depth to reduce visual fatigue, eliminate cognitive clutter, and promote sustained focus alongside physiological equilibrium.

The interface evokes quiet confidence, cognitive clarity, and vitality. Rich obsidian grounds the canvas, while luminous emerald and calming teal accents breathe life and feedback into the student's workflow without generating aggressive distraction. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric & Calming:** Deep slate tones paired with subtle light diffusion simulate a quiet, distraction-free study environment at night.
- **Vibrant Precision:** Luminous accents surface health metrics, biological rhythms, and deadline pressures through balanced visual hierarchy.
- **Glassmorphic Tactility:** Translucent surfaces, edge-refracting hairline borders, and ambient colored glows deliver physical depth and spatial separation across data-dense dashboards.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered specifically for low-light environments, prioritizing circadian ergonomics, zero eye strain, and clear semantic differentiation:

- **Primary (`#10B981` — Luminous Emerald):** Represents cognitive stamina, completed milestones, optimal biometric zones, and primary actions. It radiates vital energy against dark substrates.
- **Secondary (`#14B8A6` — Calming Teal):** Balances primary emerald with a cooler, analytical hue. Used for progress rings, secondary workflows, active focus sessions, and navigational highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` — Soothing Violet):** Reserved for deep-work states, evening sleep buffers, cognitive rest metrics, and secondary focus categories to introduce tonal sophistication.
- **Neutral (`#090D16` — Deep Obsidian Slate):** Acts as the foundational void. It contains slight blue-slate undertones to avoid the harshness of pure `#000000`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers, focal metrics, and brand identity with **Inter** for dense analytical data, task descriptions, and interface controls. 

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric contours with softened terminals produce an approachable yet authoritative tone. Tighter tracking at display scales prevents visual sprawl on dashboards.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and structurally rigid to maintain reading comfort across prolonged study sessions and detailed schedule inspection.
- **Numeric Rhythms (`metric-display`):** High-impact numerical values (e.g., focus minutes, heart-rate variability, task completion velocities) use tabular lining figures to ensure visual stability during live timers and metric rollups.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by strict 8pt proportional rhythms, adapting across mobile, tablet, and widescreen environments:

- **Desktop & Widescreen (1280px+):** 12-column structure with `margin` (2rem / 32px) and `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Accommodates a triple-pane arrangement: persistent navigation/wellness telemetry (3 columns), primary work stream and schedule (6 columns), and contextual assistant/timer dock (3 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters. The wellness panel shifts to a collapsable top drawer or bottom sheet, prioritizing the central calendar and task matrix.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile` (1rem / 16px) and `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px). All dashboard modules stack vertically with sticky quick-action widgets for timer toggles and biometric check-ins.

Component layout operates on `space-xs` through `space-2xl` scales to guarantee consistent internal breathing room for cards and navigation bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial separation relies on optical diffusion, backdrop filtration, and subtle neon backlights rather than heavy opaque drop shadows.

1. **Backdrop Filtration:** Translucent surface cards use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`, capturing subtle hues from underlying gradients while preserving text contrast.
2. **Hairline Luminescence:** Containers feature a 1px border with a directional gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25), rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.05) 50%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03))`. This creates a tactile, chiseled-glass highlight along top-left edges.
3. **Ambient Radiant Glows:** Floating active states (such as active Pomodoro counters or high-priority health cards) project diffuse aura glows using tinted drop shadows: `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.22)`.
4. **Z-Index Tiers:**
   - Level 0: Canvas substrate (`#090D16`)
   - Level 1: Flat glass cards (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`)
   - Level 2: Interactive elements, hover states, and popovers (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`)
   - Level 3: Modal dialogs, HUD timers, and global notifications (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.95)`)

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, rounded geometries with specialized curves tailored to large dashboard cards:

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form controls, text input fields, dropdown menus, and list item selections.
- **Large Corner Radius (`1rem` / 16px):** Compact overview widgets, inner modular panes, and dialogue containers.
- **Extra-Large Dashboard Radius (`1.25rem` – `1.5rem` / 20px–24px):** Primary container panels, glassmorphic wellness summaries, and timetable modules, delivering a signature modern slate appearance.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Status badges, biometric pills, tag chips, and primary floating action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with high-luminance emerald gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #10B981, #059669)`), text `#090D16` in semi-bold for maximum contrast. Glow shadow on hover (`0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Glass:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill with 1px border (`rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.3)`), text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `rounded-2xl` boundaries, multi-layered backdrop blur (16px), and obsidian slate tinting. Header areas separate cleanly with 1px hairline dividers or subtle 12px vertical spacing tokens.

### Metric Chips & Status Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags displaying current focus mode (e.g., "Deep Focus", "Rest State"). Built using tinted translucent fills (e.g., Emerald at 12% opacity) paired with high-contrast text and a pulsing 6px status dot.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Dark translucent background (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`) paired with inset borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`). On focus, border transitions smoothly to luminous teal (`#14B8A6`) with a delicate outer ring glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded squares (checkboxes) and circles (radios) styled in low-opacity slate borders. Checked states fill with the emerald-teal gradient and a bright check icon, providing unambiguous visual feedback for completed study tasks.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Biometric Focus Barometer:** A radial or progress capsule combining teal and violet gradients to visualize focus capacity versus cognitive exhaustion.
- **Session HUD (Heads-Up Display):** Minimalist floating timer anchored at the screen edge, showing active study duration with subtle perimeter breath-animation rings.
